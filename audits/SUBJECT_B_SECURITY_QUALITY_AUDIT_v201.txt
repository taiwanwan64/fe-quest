Story/template duplication audit
--------------------------------
Titles are unique and incident titles are unique.
No substantive duplicate pair was found.

There are intentional neighboring skills, but the learner decisions remain different:
- logs / exfiltration / signed_update all use operational evidence, but respectively test
  suspicious account access, insider/data removal, and endpoint incident escalation/evidence.
- access / crypto_choice both involve permissions, but access repairs an over-broad current
  permission while crypto_choice tests lifecycle removal after contract completion.
- ransomware / usb_malware / signed_update all contain incident-response elements, but their
  initial evidence and required control decisions are different.

This is useful reinforcement rather than a template with only names or constants changed.
