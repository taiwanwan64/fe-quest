FE QUEST PWA v103
Distractor plausibility / giveaway-cue audit

Purpose
- Remove answer-position clues created by obviously incomplete distractors such as “○○だけ確認する”, “○○だけ変更する”, or extreme wording such as “必ず / 常に / 一切 / 無条件”.
- Preserve one clearly best answer, while making incorrect answers plausible enough that the learner must understand priority, scope, timing, mechanism, or purpose.

Design rule introduced in v103
For judgment / case-based questions, a wrong choice should preferably be wrong for one of these reasons:
1. Priority: a reasonable action, but not the first or most important action.
2. Scope: a useful check, but too narrow for the question’s objective.
3. Timing: the action is useful, but carried out too late / too early.
4. Mechanism: a related technology or process is chosen, but it solves a different problem.
5. Responsibility / target: the action is directed at the wrong layer, party, or data.
6. Trade-off: a locally reasonable measure creates a larger risk or misses a required control.

Avoid as distractor-writing shortcuts
- “○○だけを確認する”
- “○○だけ変更する”
- “○○だけで判断する”
- “必ず / 常に / 一切 / 無条件 / 絶対” used merely to make a choice obviously false
- “何もしない”, “全て無視する” style choices unless the exam concept itself specifically requires that contrast

Notes
- “だけ” is not banned from Japanese prose globally. It can be semantically necessary in definitions or explanations. The validator targets distractor patterns where the word itself would reveal that an action is intentionally incomplete.
- The goal is not to make every incorrect choice nearly correct. The goal is to make the learner reject it for subject-matter reasons rather than for test-writing tells.
